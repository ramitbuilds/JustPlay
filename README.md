# JustPlay

### What if your next game started with a sentence?

A race through a rainy city. A greenhouse pinball table. A tiny adventure with an unlikely hero.

JustPlay turns an idea into a small, playable 2D game—and lets you keep shaping it with words. No blank project to set up. No account to create. Just a prompt, a little curiosity, and something new to try.

**[Play with your imagination →](https://justplay-olive-eta.vercel.app/)**

## From “what if” to “one more try”

1. **Describe the game.** Give it a setting, a goal and a few rules. Small, specific ideas make the best starting point.
2. **Watch it take shape.** Follow the generation stages, estimated progress and wait time as AI creates your prototype and the app checks its controls.
3. **Play a round.** Try the keyboard, touch or pointer controls shown with your game.
4. **Change your mind.** Ask for a different challenge, a new mood or an adjustment to the rules. Earlier versions stay available.
5. **Keep your favorites.** Save games in your browser, or export a copy to bring back later.

You might start with:

> “A top-down driving challenge through a forest. Collect coins, avoid rocks and reach a clear target.”

Or:

> “A beach ball game where I bounce the ball into colorful targets. Give me three lives and a score to beat.”

Be specific about what winning looks like. Then play, notice what feels good, and ask for the next change.

## A playground for ideas

JustPlay is for the moments when you want to try a game idea before it becomes a big project. It puts creation, play and iteration in the same place: describe something, test it, remix it and discover where it goes.

Games are generated by AI from your request. Reusable sprite artwork helps bring their worlds to life. Each game comes with its own instructions and controls, with options to pause, restart, enable sound and play fullscreen.

Your library lives in your browser. There is no account or cloud synchronization, so export anything you want to keep across devices.

## An MVP, with room to grow

**JustPlay is an early experiment, not a finished game studio.** AI can misunderstand a prompt, simplify an ambitious idea, produce awkward rules or introduce glitches. The app checks rendering and control responses and can attempt a repair, but those checks cannot guarantee that every game is accurate, balanced or fun.

Generation usually takes around **1–2 minutes**; retries and repairs can take longer. The displayed percentage and ETA are estimates. Provider availability and free usage limits can also interrupt a request.

If a game feels off, restart it, ask for one clear change, try an earlier version or start with a smaller idea. That feedback-and-revision loop is part of the experience.
